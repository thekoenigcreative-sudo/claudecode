The new trading simulator is built and running in the Practice Lab.

What's now running: every night and weekend (market closed only), the AI trader - Opus 5.5 - trades a real account in a proper simulation of the ASX: every stock minute by minute, the news as it was released, its own orders, stops and alerts, real costs (IBKR's brokerage, the spread, slippage). Alongside it, a search that keeps inventing and testing strategies, cheap rules-only ones first, and only puts the AI on the ones that already look promising. Every idea is logged, winners and losers.

Each live paper day is also checked against the simulator, so we know it matches what really happens.

Your first scoreboard comes Sunday evening with the lab's weekly summary: the AI trader against the simple strategies and the old rules, plus the answer to "does the AI's choosing rescue the day trader's setups?".

Nothing goes near real money; only a strategy that passes WINNER.md in full comes to you, and you decide.
